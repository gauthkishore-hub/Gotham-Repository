A couple of notes on what changed, per the actual JetRacer LaTrax build docs:

  • Jetson Orin Nano voltage: confirmed 9-20V via the DC barrel jack (not 5-9V as before) — this matches NVIDIA's official carrier board spec.
  • The multiplexer sits between the RC receiver / PCA9685 and the ESC/servo. RC receiver channel 3 goes to SEL on the mux, which picks whether channels 1/2 (throttle/steering) come from the receiver (manual) or from the Jetson's PCA9685 channels 0/1 (autonomous). The mux's OUT1/OUT2 then drive the servo and ESC respectively.
  • Power-wise, the multiplexer, receiver, and servo all ride on the ESC's regulated BEC 5V rail, separate from the Jetson's compute-side power.
